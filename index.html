WHY THIS IS NOT A CHATBOT

Conversation is only the entry point

Conversational chat

Helps someone exchange messages. A conversation by itself does not create a clinician-reviewable workflow, record state, or evidence trail.

ClinSaar structured intake

Retains patient-reported information, labels simulated assistance and document evidence, asks for patient confirmation, routes a draft to a doctor queue, and requires clinician verification before a FHIR-ready export.
